Model methodology

Cards compare live sportsbook quotes with the role-adjusted/baseline shadow projection. A model gap is not expected value, a probability, or a guarantee. The live board requires two sportsbook confirmations of the same standard main line. Top Picks shows one primary signal per player; All Props retains additional markets. Historical hit rates are descriptive, not expected value.

PropEdge is a research tool. Confirm game status, injuries, offered lines, odds, and timestamps before relying on any signal.
